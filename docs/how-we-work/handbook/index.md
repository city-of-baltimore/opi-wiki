# Staff Guide

Plain-language guidance for joining OPI and working well with other people.

These pages explain OPI practices. City policy, official forms, applicable law,
and collective bargaining agreements control when they differ. Personnel
records, benefits details, access instructions, and other controlled working
records stay in the City system that owns them.

## Pages in this section

- [Onboarding](onboarding/index.md): what new employees can expect and how
  managers prepare.
- [Working at OPI](operations/index.md): staff norms, shared-work practices,
  performance expectations, and leadership commitments.
